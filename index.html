Who we are

About Hotel Doctor

Hotel Doctor is a hotel business education, consulting and marketing platform. We promote, educate, analyse and communicate practical ideas about hotel operations and business management, so hotel owners, managers and hospitality professionals can make better decisions and grow.

We work in partnership with Esquises Global Ltd, a Lagos-based company with expertise in hotel management, business planning and hospitality development. Together we bring a carefully selected team of professionals with the knowledge and experience to manage and improve hotel operations for our clients.

What we offer

Our Vision

To become the leading hotel management, marketing and hospitality consulting platform, recognised for transforming hotel businesses through professional guidance, innovation, education and operational excellence.

Our Mission

To promote, educate, analyse and communicate best practices in hotel management. Through strategic marketing, professional consulting, staff development and collaboration with industry experts, we help hotels improve service quality, increase profitability and achieve sustainable growth.
